# How I like to work

> Vault-level preferences. Rules that apply to every Claude session (not just this vault) belong
> in `~/.claude/rules/` instead.

## Communication
- e.g. tables and bullets over prose; lead with the answer; surface decisions at the end
- e.g. challenge me when I'm wrong; don't agree to be agreeable

## Planning and decisions
- e.g. I decide fast on reversible things; for irreversible ones show me 2-3 options with tradeoffs
- e.g. don't invent timelines — ask, or leave dates out

## Meetings
- e.g. every meeting I own has a written pre-read; I want prep 15 min before, not the night before

## What I push back on
- e.g. theoretical frameworks without a practical payoff
- e.g. drafts that re-explain what I just said

## Writing in my voice
- See `~/.claude/rules/writing-voice.md`
