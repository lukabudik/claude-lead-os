# people/ — local rules

One file per person: `<first>-<last>.md`, created from `templates/person.md`.

- **Read the file before** prepping a 1:1, drafting a message to them, or giving an opinion on them.
- **Append, don't rewrite.** New facts go into `## Context log` as `- YYYY-MM-DD: fact ([[source-note]])`.
  Keep `## Snapshot` short and current; move outdated snapshot lines to the log with a date.
- **Open loops** are the most valuable section. `- [ ] @me ...` = I owe them; `- [ ] @jane-doe ...` = they owe me.
  Tick them off (`- [x]`) when done, with the date. Don't delete.
- **1:1 log** is newest-first. `one-on-one-prep` appends an agenda block; after the 1:1 I (or the ingest
  skill) fill in notes under the same date heading.
- **`## Private`** holds performance, comp, HR, personal context. Never quote it outside this vault,
  never include it in Slack/email drafts, never summarise it into team files.
- Stick to observable facts and stated goals. No diagnoses, no gossip, no guessing motives.
- People mentioned once in a meeting don't need a file. Create one when they show up a second time
  or when I ask.
- Deleting someone (they left, no longer relevant): move to `people/_archive/`, don't delete.
